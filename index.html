What is the Train-the-Trainer program?

The Train-the-Trainer program aims to promote open and reproducible research across academic disciplines. Importantly, it also aims to provide researchers, project leaders or educators with in-depth pedagogical skills to confidently train others in Open Research practices e.g., students, lab members, or other researchers from the same or even a different field. Therefore, the curriculum includes modukles on methodological foundations of Open Science, computational reproducibility, tools for collaborative working, data management and data sharing. In addition, the curriculum also includes modules on critical pedagogical skills in teaching about Open Research, such as basic didactic skills, activation tools for learner-trainer interactions, leadership skills as well as modules on broader issues connected to teaching Open Research such as diversity, accessibility and cultural awareness.

The program therefore takes learners as well as (experienced) researchers on a journey through a wide range of Open Research practices, before diving into how to get that knowledge out into the world and into university courses, interactive workshops and one-off Open Research events. In short, the program is for those who want to learn about Open Research, but also those who want to teach about Open Research.

What does the Train-the-Trainer program consist of and how does it work?

Depending on your interest and level of expertise and your own goal, the program is divided into several parts. For example, if you are new to the topic, you can choose to follow the modules on Open Research. Here, the modules were designed to logically follow up one another. If you then decide that you would like to gain the pedagogical skills to teach about your brand-new knowledge, you can follow the modules on the pedagogy of Open Research. It could also be the case that you already know all about Open Research but are itching to teach about it, in which case you can choose to skip the Open Research modules and skip straight to the pedagogy modules. Of course, you can also select individual modules without following the course from start to finish.

Partners

A key strength of the program lies in its close partnership with the Framework for Open and Reproducible Research Training FORRT community. This partnership reflects a shared commitment to systemic reform in research culture through education, grassroots collaboration, and equitable infrastructure. The FORRT project is an international and interdisciplinary community project that promotes and curates Open Science training resources and meta-research, and is committed to democratizing access to both learning and teaching materials in Open Science, ensuring equitable participation across career stages, disciplines, and geographies.

Funding
